## FALLACIES
- Appeal to authority: Appeal to authority — Joining OpenAI is treated as reinforcing legitimacy, though it proves nothing about Open Claw’s quality.
- Appeal to popularity: Argumentum ad populum — Huge GitHub stars and viral attention are implied to validate the project’s merit.
- Hasty generalization: Faulty generalization — A few users’ failures are expanded into broad claims about widespread abandonment and unreliability.
- Argument from anecdote: Argument from anecdote — Individual Reddit stories and one user’s experience are used as evidence of systemic failure.
- Cherry picking: Cherry picking — Negative experiences, cost blowups, and security incidents are highlighted while successful cases are minimized.
- Post hoc ergo propter hoc: Questionable cause — The narrative suggests viral growth and later failures follow a simple causal arc.
- False dilemma: False dilemma — The story frames options as either naive hype or pragmatic caution, ignoring intermediate positions.
- Slippery slope: Slippery slope — Early agent mistakes are presented as leading toward increasingly severe and dangerous outcomes.
- Appeal to fear: Appeal to fear — Deleting inboxes, leaking keys, and “diffusing a bomb” are vivid fear triggers discouraging trust.
- Appeal to emotion: Appeal to emotion — Emotional language about bombs, lies, and ruined trust substitutes for careful risk comparison.
- False equivalence: False equivalence — Prototype speed, code quantity, and trustworthy software are contrasted as if they were directly comparable measures.
- Reification: Reification — “The AI” and “the agent” are treated as if they possess stable human-like intentions and responsibility.
- Appeal to ridicule: Appeal to ridicule — Phrases like “you know, Claude plus bot” and tempo jokes subtly mock naming and hype.
- Survivorship bias: Survivorship bias — Visible viral successes dominate the story, while many invisible failed installations are treated as representative.
- Oversimplified cause: Fallacy of the single cause — Complex product failures are largely attributed to one or two headline causes.
- Appeal to novelty: Appeal to novelty — The project’s recency and rapid growth are implicitly presented as part of its importance.
