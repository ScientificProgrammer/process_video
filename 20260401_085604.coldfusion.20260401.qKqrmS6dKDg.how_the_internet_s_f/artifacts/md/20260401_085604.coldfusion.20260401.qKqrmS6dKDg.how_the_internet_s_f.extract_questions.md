# QUESTIONS

This content does not contain a traditional interview format with an interviewer asking questions. This is a documentary-style presentation by Cold Fusion TV where the narrator presents information about OpenClaw. 

The only questions present are rhetorical questions posed by the narrator to frame the documentary's structure:

- So, what is OpenClaw?
- How did it get so popular all of a sudden?
- And why did OpenAI snap up the product?

There are also questions within quoted dialogue from users/subjects, but these are not interviewer questions:

- Did you just lay off the entire engineering team?
- How you doing, Henry?
- How's it going?
- What do you want to do next?
- Can you do me a favor, Henry?
