# RECOMMENDATIONS

- Deploy combat strike drones only when airspace superiority is established or contested.
- Use loitering munitions when targets are exposed and timing windows are predictable.
- Mass-produce low-cost FPV drones to enable high-volume deployment despite losses.
- Develop fiber-optic controlled drones to maintain control under electronic jamming conditions.
- Reserve stealth drones for high-value targets in heavily defended airspace only.
- Protect surveillance drones with air defense or deploy in uncontested airspace.
- Implement redundant communication systems to maintain swarm drone coordination under jamming.
- Deploy swarm drones in sufficient numbers to saturate and overwhelm enemy defenses.
- Keep naval drones low-profile and undetected until final approach to target.
- Position electronic warfare drones close to enemy systems for maximum disruption effectiveness.
- Maintain high-altitude drones beyond range of enemy air defense capabilities.
- Build algorithmic safeguards and human oversight into autonomous drone decision-making systems.
- Secure cargo drone routes with air superiority before establishing supply corridors.
- Layer counter-drone systems to prevent saturation by multiple simultaneous threats.
- Invest in anti-jamming technology to protect drone control signals and GPS.
- Develop adaptive electronic warfare systems that evolve faster than enemy countermeasures.
- Match drone type to operational environment and threat level precisely.
- Establish backup control methods for all drone types in case primary fails.
- Deploy multiple drone types simultaneously to force enemies into complex defense decisions.
- Prioritize rapid replacement capability over individual drone survivability for expendable systems.
