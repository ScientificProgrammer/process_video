### Headwinds

#### Industry Challenges

**Ethical and Moral Opposition**
- Significant public fear of "Terminator/Skynet" scenarios
- AI experts "pleading with countries to slow down before it's too late"
- Concerns about algorithms making life-or-death decisions
- Professor Toby Walsh warning: "It will look like a bad Hollywood movie"

**Dual-Use Technology Dilemma**
- Same AI systems for disaster response can be weaponized
- Professor Walsh: "Everything you do can be taken by other people and used for purposes that you wouldn't like"
- No meaningful distinction between civilian rescue robots and battlefield robots
- Chinese-made robot dog ($4,000) demonstrates technology accessibility to adversaries

**Catastrophic Failure Risk**
- Worst case scenario: "Nuclear conflict that was unnecessary, unwanted, that it was a mistake that the algorithms made"
- Loss of human control over decision-making
- Potential for autonomous weapons to make deadly miscalculations
- Example: Russian Poseidon drone "at the whims of an algorithm decide to blow up half of Sydney"

**Proliferation Concerns**
- Technology spreading to adversaries rapidly
- China has "the lead in a lot of AI relevant technology. They're making them cheaper. They're making them better."
- Iran's reported 80,000 drone stockpile
- Race dynamics creating pressure to deploy before full safety validation

#### Market Constraints

**Public Trust Deficit**
- Widespread fear of autonomous weapons systems
- Comparison to dystopian science fiction narratives
- Concern that "this is actually making the world a less safe place"
- Resistance to "changing the nature of conflict in a way that's going to make it worse for all of us"

**Regulatory Uncertainty**
- Potential for future restrictions on autonomous weapons
- International calls to "hold back from using these weapons...developing these weapons...selling these weapons"
- Unclear accountability frameworks for AI-caused casualties
- Ethical guidelines still being developed

**Market Education Requirements**
- Need to overcome "science fiction" perceptions
- Complex technology requiring customer education
- Accountability questions: "Where do you draw that line to ensure safety?"
- Resistance from those who believe autonomous weapons lower barriers to war
