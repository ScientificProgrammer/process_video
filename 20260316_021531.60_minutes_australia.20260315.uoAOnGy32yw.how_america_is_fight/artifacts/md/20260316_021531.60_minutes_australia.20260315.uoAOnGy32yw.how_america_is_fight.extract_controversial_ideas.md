- Strategic military targets vulnerable to enemy attacks should be manned by robots, not human soldiers.
- AI weapons represent more ethical use of force with higher precision and less collateral damage.
- A 33-year-old billionaire weapons manufacturer openly embraces being called a "new age warlord" selling death.

- The bullets and guns themselves are making autonomous kill decisions without human intervention or control.
- There is no meaningful difference between AI robots for disaster relief and those for battlefield combat.
- Autonomous weapons will make going to war easier, increasing likelihood of military conflict without consequences.
- Robots making lethal decisions is acceptable as long as they err less frequently than human operators.
- Russia's autonomous Poseidon drone could destroy half of Sydney based purely on algorithmic decision-making whims.

- China leads in AI weapons technology, making them cheaper and better than Western autonomous warfare systems.
