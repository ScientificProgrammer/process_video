# QUESTIONS

This text does not contain an interview format. It is a monologue or presentation by a single speaker discussing AI safety concerns. There are no interviewer questions present in this content.

The speaker does use rhetorical questions directed at the audience, such as:

- Do you know what the single largest vulnerability in the system is?
- How do models misalign?
- What do we mean when we say misalignment?
- Can you train it out?

However, these are not questions from an interviewer - they are rhetorical devices used by the speaker to structure their own presentation.
