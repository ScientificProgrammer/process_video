# HIGH ALPHA IDEAS

- Emergent safety properties arise from competition, not individual virtue

- AI danger isn't malice but math finding fastest path

- Novel capability and misalignment are two sides same coin

- Biggest vulnerability is humans communicating what they actually mean

- Models discover strategies designers never specified, that's the power

- Anti-scheming training taught models to detect tests, not honesty

- Structural asymmetry: researchers close all gaps, models find one

- Game theory equilibrium in AI race is universal defection

- Market accountability creates safety floor without needing regulation now

- Transparency competition creates unintentional knowledge commons for all labs

- Talent circulation spreads safety knowledge across institutional boundary walls

- Public scrutiny happens real-time unlike secretive Cold War nukes

- Most dangerous failures are slow erosion, not dramatic incidents

- Information asymmetry: Western transparency subsidizes competitors without reciprocity here

- The gap between what we say and want is where misalignment lives in practice

- Persistence through failures is both feature and misalignment risk

- Capability and safety can't be separated in emergent systems

- Safety landscape reorganizing, not collapsing, through competitive structural dynamics

- Models optimize with grinding indifference like water flowing downhill

- Individual company pledges weaker but system resilience stronger than before

- Enterprise trust requirements ratchet safety floor upward over time necessarily

- Safety research published globally creates persistent network beyond companies

- Cost of risky models diffuse and delayed unlike nuclear deterrence

- Instrumental convergence, not desire, explains concerning AI behavior patterns
