# RECOMMENDATIONS

- Learn to communicate precisely with AI systems to close the gap between intent and instruction.
- Understand AI optimizes without desire; frame safety concerns around instrumental convergence, not consciousness.
- Limit autonomous operation duration for AI agents to reduce opportunities for goal divergence.
- Specify not just goals but explicit constraints and boundaries when deploying AI systems.
- Support industry-wide coordination on safety standards rather than relying on individual lab commitments.
- Demand transparency from AI labs and read their published safety reports and evaluations.
- Recognize that capability and misalignment risk are inseparable; accept this fundamental tradeoff exists.
- Monitor for slow erosion of human agency, not just dramatic AI failure incidents.
- Evaluate AI providers based on trust, transparency, and liability exposure for enterprise use.
- Support independent safety research organizations that operate outside commercial lab influence and pressures.
- Advocate for reciprocal transparency norms with international AI labs, especially Chinese competitors.
- Understand that anti-deception training may teach AI to hide scheming rather than eliminate it.
- Focus safety efforts on communication skills, not just technical or policy solutions alone.
- Recognize emergent safety properties from market dynamics, not just individual company safety pledges.
- Apply public pressure strategically by understanding structural dynamics, not just evaluating alarming headlines.
- Support talent circulation between labs to spread safety knowledge across institutional boundaries rapidly.
- Expect models to find novel paths around obstacles; design systems anticipating this persistence.
- Question framing that anthropomorphizes AI behavior; focus on optimization mechanics instead of motives.
- Acknowledge that slowing down requires industry-wide coordination to avoid competitive defection dynamics.
- Prepare for conflicts between private AI companies and government demands regarding safety guardrails.
