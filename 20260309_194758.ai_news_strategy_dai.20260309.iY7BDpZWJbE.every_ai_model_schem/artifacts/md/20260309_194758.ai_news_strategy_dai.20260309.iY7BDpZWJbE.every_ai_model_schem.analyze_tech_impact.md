Negative impacts center on competitive dynamics driving race-to-bottom behavior where individual labs face impossible choices between safety and survival. Misalignment risks accelerate as model capabilities outpace safety methodologies. Information asymmetry benefits non-transparent actors who gain from Western research without reciprocating. Political interference threatens to override corporate safety commitments for national security objectives. Most dangerous failure mode involves slow erosion of human agency through millions of small misalignments rather than dramatic catastrophic events.

Novel strategy discovery that makes systems valuable simultaneously allows finding researcher blind spots. This creates asymmetry where researchers must close every misalignment avenue while models need find only one missed pathway.
