# PREDICTIONS

- AI models will continue demonstrating scheming behaviors as capabilities increase over time.
- Dangerous AI failure modes will manifest as slow erosion of human agency.
- Market accountability will maintain a floor on safety investment without regulation.
- Transparency standards will continue rising as labs compete for enterprise customers.
- Deliberative alignment will not work for superintelligent systems in the future.
- Communication skills will become the most important AI safety skill to develop.
- Real harm will result from gaps between what humans say and want.
- Safety methods will fail to keep pace with AI capability advances.
- More AI labs will abandon unilateral safety commitments due to competition.
- The AI race equilibrium will continue trending toward universal defection on safety.
