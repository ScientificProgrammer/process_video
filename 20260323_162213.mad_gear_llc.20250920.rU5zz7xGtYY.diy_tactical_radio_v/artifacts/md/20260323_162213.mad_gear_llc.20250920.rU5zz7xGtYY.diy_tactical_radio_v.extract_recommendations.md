# RECOMMENDATIONS

- Avoid buying military-grade MANET radios unless issued for your job or mission-critical needs.
- Consider Open MANET for community resilience instead of expensive proprietary military solutions.
- Assess your actual data throughput needs before investing in high-end equipment.
- Visit the Open MANET GitHub to access free information and project documentation.
- Start building your own MANET system for around one hundred dollars.
- Use Raspberry Pi as a testbed for developing DIY MANET capabilities.
- Implement OpenWRT and Batman Advanced software for intelligent mesh network routing.
- Explore Wi-Fi HaLow technology for extended range in the 900 MHz band.
- Join the Open MANET community to contribute time, energy, and development efforts.
- Design and share 3D-printed enclosures for open-source MANET hardware projects.
- Recognize that standard Wi-Fi security is sufficient unless facing nation-state threats.
- Accept limitations of DIY systems while understanding they meet most use cases.
- Support open-source projects to drive innovation and make technology more accessible.
- Tinker and experiment with MANET technology to push limits and gain knowledge.
- Understand mesh networks self-heal when nodes continuously evaluate neighbor connection quality.
- Use 1-15 megabytes per second for situational awareness, pictures, and map updates.
- Leverage ATAK software for tactical data sharing on community resilience networks.
- Contribute awareness by sharing information about Open MANET with interested communities.
- Be realistic about whether you need hundreds of nodes or just community-scale networking.
- Participate in open-source communities to democratize expensive technology for everyone.
