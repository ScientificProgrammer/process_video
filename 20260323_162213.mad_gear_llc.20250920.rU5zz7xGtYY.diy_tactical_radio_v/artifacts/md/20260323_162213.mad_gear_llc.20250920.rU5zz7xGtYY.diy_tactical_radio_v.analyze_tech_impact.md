# TECHNOLOGY IMPACT ANALYSIS

## SUMMARY

Open MANET is a community-driven open-source project developing affordable Mobile Ad Hoc Networking radio systems for civilian resilience using accessible hardware and software.

## TECHNOLOGIES USED

- OpenWRT (Linux-based networking operating system)
- Batman Advanced (Better Approach To Mobile Ad hoc Networking protocol)
- Wi-Fi HaLow (802.11ah standard operating at 900 MHz)
- Raspberry Pi single-board computers
- IEEE 802.11s mesh networking standard
- 3D-printed enclosures
- Standard Wi-Fi security protocols

## TARGET AUDIENCE

- Community resilience groups and emergency preparedness organizations
- DIY technology enthusiasts and makers
- Individuals seeking affordable mesh networking solutions
- Open-source development communities
- Civilians requiring reliable local communications infrastructure without military-grade budgets

## OUTCOMES

- Provides MANET capabilities at approximately $100 versus $20,000 military-grade alternatives
- Enables 1-15 megabytes per second data throughput sufficient for situational awareness applications
- Supports multiple nodes for community-scale mesh networks with self-healing capabilities
- Creates accessible documentation and recipes through GitHub for DIY implementation
- Demonstrates viable alternative to proprietary military systems for civilian applications
- Facilitates picture sharing, data packages, map updates, and single video streaming
- Extends communication range to miles versus hundreds of feet with conventional Wi-Fi

## SOCIETAL IMPACT

Positive Impacts:

Open MANET democratizes access to resilient communication technology previously available only to military and well-funded organizations. By reducing costs from five figures to approximately $100, the project enables community-based emergency preparedness and disaster response capabilities. The open-source nature promotes knowledge sharing, skill development, and technological literacy among civilians. Communities can establish independent communication infrastructure that functions when traditional networks fail, enhancing local resilience and self-sufficiency.

The project fosters innovation by creating a middle-market solution space, potentially spurring commercial development of affordable MANET products. It builds technical communities where individuals collaborate, learn, and contribute to collective knowledge. The transparency of open-source development allows for community oversight and continuous improvement.

Negative Impacts:

The DIY nature means systems lack the reliability, testing, and ruggedization of professional equipment, potentially creating false confidence in emergency situations. Users may overestimate capabilities or underestimate limitations, leading to communication failures during critical moments. The technology requires technical knowledge that may exclude less technically-inclined community members, potentially creating digital divides within communities.

Without professional support infrastructure, troubleshooting and maintenance fall entirely on users. The reliance on volunteer development means inconsistent progress, potential security vulnerabilities, and lack of guaranteed updates or support. Standard Wi-Fi security, while adequate for most civilian uses, could be compromised by sophisticated actors, potentially exposing community communications.

## ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

The Open MANET project presents minimal ethical concerns. The primary consideration involves ensuring users understand system limitations to prevent over-reliance during emergencies. Transparency about capabilities versus military-grade systems is essential to prevent misplaced confidence that could endanger lives.

There exists potential dual-use concern, as communication technologies can be employed for both beneficial community resilience and potentially harmful coordination of illegal activities. However, this risk is inherent to all communication technologies and does not uniquely characterize this project.

The open-source nature promotes ethical values of transparency, accessibility, and knowledge democratization. The project explicitly positions itself as community resilience infrastructure rather than tactical military equipment, maintaining appropriate civilian focus.

Privacy considerations exist with any mesh networking system, requiring clear communication about data transmission and potential interception. Standard Wi-Fi security provides reasonable protection for civilian applications but requires user awareness of limitations.

Ethical Concern Severity: LOW

The project demonstrates responsible development with appropriate transparency about capabilities and limitations, focusing on civilian community benefit rather than militarization or harmful applications.

## SUSTAINABILITY

Environmental Perspective:

Open MANET demonstrates moderate environmental sustainability. The project utilizes existing consumer hardware like Raspberry Pi computers, reducing specialized manufacturing demands. However, reliance on electronics contributes to e-waste concerns, particularly as technology evolves and older components become obsolete. The 3D-printed enclosures, while ingenious, typically use plastic materials with environmental implications. The project could improve sustainability by emphasizing repairable designs, component longevity, and recycling protocols.

Energy consumption appears modest compared to commercial alternatives, as the systems use low-power components designed for efficiency. The 900 MHz frequency provides extended range, potentially reducing the number of nodes required for coverage, thereby decreasing overall material and energy footprint.

Economic Perspective:

Economic sustainability is strong. The approximately $100 price point makes the technology accessible to individuals and communities with limited budgets, representing a 200-fold cost reduction compared to military alternatives. This accessibility enables widespread adoption without significant financial barriers.

However, the volunteer-driven development model presents economic sustainability challenges. Without funding mechanisms, development pace depends entirely on contributor availability and personal investment. This could limit long-term viability unless sustainable funding models emerge, such as donations, grants, or commercial partnerships that maintain open-source principles.

The project creates economic value by building technical skills within communities, potentially enabling local employment opportunities in network deployment and maintenance. It also pressures the market to develop mid-range commercial solutions, potentially spurring economic activity.

Social Perspective:

Social sustainability is the project's strongest dimension. By democratizing access to resilient communications, Open MANET empowers communities to build self-reliant infrastructure. The collaborative development model builds social capital through shared learning and contribution.

The project's longevity depends on maintaining active community engagement. Open-source projects can fade without sustained contributor interest. However, the practical application to emergency preparedness provides strong motivation for continued development and knowledge preservation.

Accessibility remains a concern, as technical barriers may exclude less technically-inclined individuals. The project would benefit from developing more user-friendly implementations and educational resources to broaden participation across diverse community members.

## SUMMARY AND RATING

Open MANET successfully democratizes resilient mesh networking for civilian communities, providing accessible alternatives to expensive military systems while maintaining realistic capability expectations and ethical development practices.

Societal Benefit Rating: HIGH

Sustainability Rating: MEDIUM
