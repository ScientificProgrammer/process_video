# QUESTIONS

This transcript does not contain a traditional interview format with an interviewer asking questions. Instead, it is a documentary-style investigative journalism piece where Karen (the journalist/narrator) presents information and includes statements from various sources (Tyler Johnston, Catherine Bracy, Rebecca Bauer-Kahan, Alex Bores, Josh Vlasto, and others).

The only rhetorical questions posed by Karen as part of her narration are:

- I wanted to know: what are AI companies so afraid of?
- And what does it mean for the rest of us if they get what they want?

These are not interview questions directed at subjects, but rather framing questions for the investigative piece itself.
