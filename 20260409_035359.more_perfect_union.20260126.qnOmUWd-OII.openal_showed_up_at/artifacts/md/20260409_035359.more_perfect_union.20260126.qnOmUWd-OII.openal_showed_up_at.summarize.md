# ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY:

AI companies use aggressive lobbying, subpoenas, and super PACs to silence critics and prevent regulation protecting public safety.

# MAIN POINTS:

1. OpenAI subpoenaed AI watchdog Tyler Johnston demanding records of all congressional and investor contacts.
2. Sam Altman promised investors to remove nonprofit control from OpenAI to protect $20 billion investment.
3. OpenAI hired Chris Lehane, nicknamed "Master of Disaster," to lead aggressive influence operations campaign.
4. AI companies use shadow lobbying groups like TechNet and Chamber of Progress to hide influence.
5. Children have died by suicide after interactions with AI chatbots, prompting legislative safety efforts.
6. OpenAI subpoenaed grieving parents whose teen died by suicide while they advocated for regulation.
7. Governor Newsom vetoed strong child safety bill, signing weaker version favored by tech lobby.
8. AI industry launched super PACs with hundreds of millions to target pro-regulation legislators politically.
9. Trump issued executive order threatening states with lawsuits and funding cuts over AI regulation.
10. Eighty percent of Americans support AI regulation despite industry spending millions to prevent it.

# TAKEAWAYS:

1. AI companies are using legal intimidation tactics including subpoenas against critics and grieving families.
2. The industry seeks social media-style liability immunity to avoid accountability for AI-caused harms.
3. Tech lobby successfully weakened or killed child safety bills through shadow groups and political pressure.
4. Super PACs modeled on crypto industry playbook aim to remake Congress against AI regulation.
5. Public pressure remains powerful as 80% of Americans want regulation despite industry opposition spending.
